## The problem

A way to explore the CHRIST University campus in 360 degrees, from a browser on a laptop or a phone.

## What I built

An immersive 360° web experience in React, Three.js and TypeScript, deployed on Vercel.

- **Interactive hotspots** on the panorama, with multimedia integration.
- **Smooth navigation controls** for moving around the campus.
- **Responsive design,** optimised for web and mobile.

## Key decisions

<Decisions>
  <Decision
    title="Three.js for the panorama"
    why="A 360° scene is a 3D problem. Three.js renders it in the browser without a plugin."
    tradeoff="WebGL scenes are heavier than static pages, so performance on phones needs care."
  />
  <Decision
    title="Hotspots as an interactive layer"
    why="Hotspots turn a passive panorama into a guided tour and keep multimedia one click away."
    tradeoff="Each hotspot is content that someone has to place and maintain."
  />
  <Decision
    title="Responsive from the start"
    why="A tour is only useful if it works wherever it's opened, on a desktop browser or a phone."
    tradeoff="Mouse and touch controls both have to feel right, which adds interaction testing."
  />
</Decisions>
